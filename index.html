Denver Police Investigate Unusual “Bicycle Seat Sniffing” Incident

Rocky Ford Man Charged with Lewd and Salacious behavior/Public Indecency

by: Juaquin Biggums
Posted: Sep 1, 2026 / 07:00 AM MDT
Updated: Sep 1, 2026 / 08:56 AM MDT

DENVER (KDVR) — Denver police responded to reports of a man allegedly hiding in bushes near a bicycle rack and behaving suspiciously around several bicycles.

The suspect, Andrew Deason, 40, of Rocky Ford, CO, was reportedly discovered after witnesses contacted authorities about the unusual behavior witnesed in the vicinity of the Cheesman Park area.

"This is certainly one of the strangest calls we've received”

— Denver Police Department Spokesperson

The report alleges that Mr. Deason who had previously been involved in other incidents involving ‘sniffing’ bicycle seats as well as other lewd and salacious behavior was seen in the vicinity of the Cheesman Park area hiding behind bushes and sniffing the bicycle seats of male parkgoers after they put their bike away in the bike rack.

"I just don’t know what is wrong with some people I mean there are children who come to this park”

— Anonymous Eyewitness

The realization that an individual is surreptitiously targeting personal property in public spaces shattered the community's baseline sense of safety and mutual trust. Beyond the immediate violation of personal boundaries and bodily autonomy, such unsettling behavior transforms everyday environments like neighborhood parks, bike racks, and school grounds into zones of hyper-vigilance. Residents are forced to compromise their daily routines, changing how and where they commute, while parents become hesitant to let children ride freely. This pervasive anxiety metastasizes, eroding public trust, discouraging outdoor activity, and burdening local authorities with complaints—effectively turning a seemingly localized act of deviance into a widespread disruption of community cohesion and peace of mind.

This investigation is ongoing and all parties should be considered innocent until proven guilty in a court of law. Any and all information pertaining to this situation should be reported to Denver Police at (303) 555-6969.
